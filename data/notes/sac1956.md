## 2. Table layout (verified on rendered pages)

**Legend from the study itself** (section1.pdf p.21, "SAMPLE PAGE - INDUSTRIAL COMPLEXES (See page 200)"). Headers: `PRI NO. | REF NO. | NAME | COORDINATES | DGZ | BONUS B.A. | [weapons columns, redacted]`. Its callouts read:
- "Major Complex Name (Plus country name, if in Satellite)"
- "Sub-Complex Name"
- "Bombing Encyclopedia Numbers - First 4 digits indicate WAC on which target is located; second 4 digits indicate installation or complex number within the WAC area"
- "Category Code Number (See Page 154)"
- "# Represents target on which SAC is to deliver weapon for that particular command and for which that command has weapon programmed"
- "X Represents target duplicated with command indicated"
- "Bought by Afld" (the B.A. column)

Study p.15 (section1 p.20): "In the Airfield List ... each column, from left to right, pertains to the following: priority number, reference number, name, DGZ, weapon types, command interest. Since all airfield weapons are surface-burst ...". "(b) In the industrial Complex List, the format is similar." "Part II follows the same format as in Part I."

**Complex list pages** (306-p file). The only printed header on list pages is `DGZ BA`. The right half is a redaction box stamped "FOIA(b)(3) - 42 USC 2168(a)(1)(C) - FRD". Each complex is a block of lines:
- Major complex header: `PRI REF NAME[ COUNTRY] DDMM-DDDMM`, e.g. `1 5150 MOSCOW 5545-03737`, `61 0685 BERLIN GER SOVZONE 5231-01324`, `668 00255 ADJUD RUM 4606-02712`. PRI is 1-4 digits. REF is 4 digits, sometimes 5 (00255, 08905, 99505), and is ordered alphabetically. No country suffix means USSR.
- DGZ lines: `DDMM- DDMME <letters>` (a space replaces the leading zero of the longitude), e.g. `5545- 3737E A`, `5304-11730E B`. Letter labels: A, B, AH, AM, BC, BM, H, J, K, AC, L, N ... They are unique within a complex but not sequential (single-DGZ complexes are usually `B`). Some complexes have no DGZ line at all (ADJUD, AGA, AKHALKALAKI, MORDOVO, ZNOJMO, ZOSSEN).
- Installation lines: `CCC WWWW-NNNN` (category code + BE number), e.g. `366 0155-0121`. The installation part is often blank (`0167-`). Population rows are `275 WWWW-9xxx` (or blank).
- Sub-complex header (indented): `NAME[ COUNTRY] DDMM-DDDMM`, optionally followed by its own DGZ line(s) and installation lines (e.g. DVIRI, KORISTOVKA, POTSDAM E GER).
- Special site lines (seen under ZVENIGOROD): `PESTOVO M-24 5540-03650 QA` (name + coordinates + Q-code in the DGZ column, no category or BE). These are probably the Moscow missile ring.

**Airfield list pages** (sections 4 and 6), one row per airfield: `PRI REF NAME[, COUNTRY] WWWW-8xxx DDMM-DDDMM L`.
- e.g. `1 1090 BYKHOV 0167-8001 5331-03012 R`.
- REF is the **parent complex's** REF: all MOSCOW/... airfields carry 5150; BRANDIS has 4465 = LEIPZIG E GER; ORANIENBURG has 0685 = BERLIN; WELZOW has 7345, matching the cross-reference "WELZOW E GER AF SEE 7345 SCHWARZHEIDE E GER".
- BE installation numbers are 8xxx.
- Coordinates have no hemisphere letter except West longitudes, e.g. `CHAPLINO 6424-17217W`.
- Trailing letter: R/S/T/U/V/Z. Its meaning is not stated on the pages viewed.
- About 27 rows per page.

**Cross-reference list** (section2): `REF NAME COUNTRY [AF] SEE <REF> <MAJOR COMPLEX>`.

**Category code list**: two columns of `code | description`, with underlined sub-headings for regional groups (electric power grids, railroad repair plants, railroad yards).

**Country codes printed**:
- Complex list: RUM, CZECH, E GER, GER SOVZONE (Berlin), POL, HUNG, BULG, ALB, CHINA, KOREA / N KOREA, VIETNAM (the last via OCR: HANOI VIETNAM, PRI 608).
- Airfield list: ", POL", ", E GER", ", CZECH", ", RUM", ", HUNG", ", CHINA", ", MANCH" (Manchuria), ", N KOREA", ", ALB", ", BULG".

## 6. Effort estimate and recommended pipeline

Line counts come from a projection profile calibrated on the pilot pages (error about ±1 line/page; 300 dpi geometry: content is the top 48% of a 1716x2464 raster, the list is in x 10-45%).
- **Part I complex list**: 305 pages, about 13,500 printed lines (mean 45, range 35-54). Applying the pilot mix (60% installation, 19% DGZ, 18% header, 3% site) gives about 8,200 category/BE rows, 2,500 DGZ lines and 2,400 complex/sub-complex headers (about 1,200 major complexes). The DGZ figure fits the study's "3400 DGZs" once the ~1,100 airfields are added.
- **Part II airfield list (complete)**: 42 pages, about 1,110 rows.
- **Optional**: section4 (10 pp, Part I airfield excerpts; same PRIs as Part II on the pages compared); section7 (24 pp, Part II complex excerpts); section2 (12 pp, cross-reference excerpts).
- Total for complete coverage: about 350 pages, about 14,600 lines, about 160k digits.
- At about 2 agent-minutes per page, one pass is about 12 agent-hours. Two independent passes plus adjudication of disagreements make about 25-30 agent-hours. It splits by page across 10 parallel workers (about 3 h each).

Pipeline:
1. pdftoppm -r 300 -gray. Crop the list region and split it into 2-3 overlapping horizontal bands so each image is near native size. Ask the vision model for a strict line-typed schema (complex_header / sub_complex_header / dgz / installation / site) with "?" for uncertain glyphs. Run **two independent passes** and diff them.
2. Automatic checks, each catching a known error class:
   - (a) line count per page vs projection count.
   - (b) regex formats; minutes < 60.
   - (c) category code in the 191-code list.
   - (d) BE WAC constant within a complex, and WAC consistent with its coordinates (learn WAC bounding boxes from the data). This would have flagged ABDULINO 0169 vs 0165.
   - (e) DGZ and sub-complex coordinates within about 1° of the complex reference point.
   - (f) exactly one 275 row per complex/sub-complex block.
   - (g) REF increases alphabetically; PRI unique, no gaps within each list (catches 483/489-type misreads).
   - (h) airfield REF must exist as a complex REF; BE installation numbers 8xxx for airfields, 9xxx for population.
   - (i) cross-check category totals against the NSA city sheets (Moscow, Leningrad, Beijing, Warsaw) and the EBB figures (Berlin 91).
3. For lines where the passes disagree or a check fails, re-read a 3x zoom of that line. Keep the printed string and mark "?" when still undecidable.
4. Write SCHEMA.md-format CSVs: one row per complex/airfield with priority, lat/lon from the printed coordinates, source_page and the printed line as the quote. Keep DGZ and installation rows in a linked long table.

## 9. Full transcription (5–7 October 2026)

The whole Part I complex list (306 PDF pages) and the Part II airfield list (43 pages) are now
transcribed: two independent passes, adjudication of every disagreement on enlarged crops, and
a second look at every line that a consistency check across lines flags. The tables, the data
card and the method are in `data/curated/sac1956/` (README.md, REPORT.md); the pipeline is
`scripts/sac1956_strips.py`, `sac1956_compare.py`, `sac1956_adjudicate_prep.py`,
`sac1956_assemble.py`; the passes and decisions are in `data/interim/sac1956/`.

What the full read showed about the document (beyond section 2):
- **PDF page 46 repeats page 45** line for line (CHERNIGOV … CHIA MU SSU MANCH); page 47
  continues page 45.
- **The scan of PDF page 64 is cut off at the foot**, after SAMBOR. Page 65 opens inside a
  Bulgarian complex (chart 0322, sub-complex GORNA ORYAKHOVITSA BULG) whose header sorted between
  DROGOBYCH and DUBNICE NAD VAHOM; it is probably priority 1054, the only number missing from
  its alphabetical tier. Every other page break continues cleanly, so no whole page is missing.
  The "p.306 = study p.504" mapping of section 1 therefore cannot hold everywhere (one page is
  duplicated); printed page numbers are visible only on the last page.
- **Priorities come in alphabetical tiers from about 321 down** (701 ABDULINO … 732 YERSHOVO,
  739 APOSTOLOVO …): ties within a tier were broken alphabetically. Above 321 the numbers rank.
- **Five-digit reference numbers** (`00255`, `13045`, `52585`) insert a complex between two
  four-digit numbers; with that reading the reference numbers rise through the whole list.
- **609 of 1,216 complexes have no DGZ line**, mostly low priorities; all North Korean, North
  Vietnamese and Albanian complexes are among them.
- **M-n rows** (35, one with an irregular coordinate) are all 44–96 km from Moscow, in two bands
  (44–64 km and 72–96 km): probably the two rings of the Moscow surface-to-air missile system.
  Rows with a reference number stand alone in the alphabet; the others follow a complex
  (e.g. DMITROV).
- **Country suffixes**: North Vietnam is confirmed (8 complexes: CAM PHA, HAI DUONG, HAIPHONG,
  HANOI, HON GAY, PORT WALLUT, THANH HOA, VINH). ULAAN BAATAR is printed with the suffix CHINA.
  No Iranian, Yugoslav or other non-bloc place is on either list (checked on the full
  transcription, not OCR). The 25-character name column cuts some suffixes (CZEC, CHIN, E GE,
  and once a bare C).
- **Airfield reference numbers** name a Part I complex for about two thirds of the airfields;
  the rest name complexes that are not in Part I.
- **The Archive's city sheets** (section 1) agree with the transcription for Warsaw, Fengtai and
  the Moscow and Leningrad suburbs; they differ by 1–6 lines for the cities themselves, and give
  Sablino's railroad yard the code 350 (Caucasus region) where the list prints 358 (Northern
  region). See REPORT.md.

Open issues from section 7 that this settles: the Iran/Yugoslavia negatives (now checked on
every page), North Vietnam (confirmed), and the population-line rule: 2,081 of the 2,089 complex
and sub-complex blocks have exactly one 275 line. SMOLENSK (priority 3), NIEDER ULLERSDORF POL
and the sub-complexes VAYENGA and DIOSGYOR HUNG have none; TULOMA and KAZINCBARCIKA HUNG have
two (DIOSGYOR's neighbour). The umbrella complexes GLIWICE-SOSNOWIEC POL and HALLE-MERSEBURG
E GER keep all their lines in sub-complexes. SMOLENSK, TULOMA and KAZINCBARCIKA were checked on
the scan: they are printed that way. Still open: the meaning of the DGZ letter labels and the airfield trailing
letters; Part II beyond the airfield list; the Wellerstein data.
