# Soviet and Warsaw Pact target material: second search, November 2026

Search date 2026-11-01, 17:39 to about 18:07 CET. This extends `soviet_search_2026-11.md`. Nothing here has been entered in `data/curated/labels/`; every row below is a candidate and needs a second reader.

## Result in brief

No new war plan naming places was found. The useful finds are of three other kinds.

1. **Two more General Staff Academy lessons give new named nuclear targets.** Lesson 27a names 21 targets, 20 in a single list on p.8, mostly in the Netherlands and Belgium, for the phase of the Rhine crossing. Lesson 24b gives four targets with a stated yield of 100 kt. These are lessons in one fictional scenario, not plans, and they extend the same source as `wp_1977_gsa_lessons`.
2. **The Mazowsze exercise booklet of 1963 is a large mirror source.** It is a one-sided Polish command-staff map exercise, not a plan. Its text gives the strike areas, and its maps place labelled burst symbols across Poland: 65 candidates read by eye, plus 3 from the text.
3. **A CIA report on a Polish Maritime Front operational plan (report dated 11 March 1968) has a first-strike table, but its targets are units, not places.** It gives no rows. It is the closest thing to a plan document opened, and is worth a human reader.

### Caveats on the lessons

All lessons belong to one scenario, so they add rows but not an independent plan. The scan pages cited were read as images; the OCR garbles them ("mmitions" for "munitions", 67,000 for 64,000). PDF page equals printed page on the pages read.

Lesson 27a p.8 is a list of "main enemy targets to be destroyed by nuclear weapons", reported by students in a role. The strikes belong to the follow-on phase around 15-19 September in the scenario, not the initial strike. Pp.21-26 give the commander's decision text. I read pp.8, 23, 25 and 26 as images; pp.9, 21, 22 and 24 were rendered but not read as images.

Target countries are not stated in the documents. I assigned them from the place names, as the second read did. The list also has unit targets located only by a phase line or none at all, which are not rows. P.25 sets five nuclear warheads and "the main forces" against the enemy "in the area of COEVORDEN, ALMELO, DEVENTER"; this is too broad and mixed for a row.

In Lesson 24b two passages on p.16 are blanked with the HR70-14 mark, and the "newly detected targets" there are a later refinement of the plan. The OCR there is also disturbed by overprinting, so I took those quotes from the image.

### Caveats on the Mazowsze maps

The kind is exercise and mirror: Polish staff writing what NATO would hit in Poland. The text calls it a "ćwiczenie dowódczo-sztabowe na mapach" (PDF p.5, printed p.9). It must not be used as evidence of NATO intent or called a plan.

The maps are the weakest material in this note:

- A symbol has no text of its own. A row means only that a burst symbol sits beside a printed label.
- The legend on Schemat 3 distinguishes "Uderz. naziemne" and "Uderz. powietrz.", but the text (printed p.23) gives 8 ground bursts on the Soviet border and 47 air bursts, while the map shows many more than 8 hatched symbols inland. So the meaning of the hatched symbol is not settled, and I do not assign burst type from it. The 8 border bursts do match the plume symbols.
- Counts: the tables give 55 strikes on Schemat 3 and 37 more on Schemat 4, against 36 in the text (1,070 kt against 1,030 kt). Schemat 5 gives 18 more and a total of 109 strikes, 3,830 kt. Named rows are far fewer than these, because many symbols are unlabelled, so the list is not complete. Teardrop symbols are not counted as bursts; I take them to be the special-purpose group drops but did not find a legend entry, so they give no rows.
- Omitted though a symbol is near a label: Tarnów on Schemat 4 (ambiguous placement), and the dense Silesian cluster on Schemat 5 (unreadable at 120 dpi). Worth a crop at 300 dpi.
- Diacritics on map labels are as best read at this resolution. "Deblin" appears to lack its ogonek.

## Method

robots.txt was read before first use of phpisn.ethz.ch (404, no rules), archive.org, www.cia.gov, warplans.org, americanstudies.history.knu.ua, nsarchive.gwu.edu and www.wilsoncenter.org. None bars this use. wilsoncenter.org asks for a 10-second crawl delay and got one document request. A named research User-Agent was used with curl and scripts. Downloads went to one directory each under `/var/tmp/np-search-4/`; scripts stayed in `/var/tmp/np-search-4-scripts/`. The PHP crawl covered 398 pages and stopped when all 18 listed ids were found.
