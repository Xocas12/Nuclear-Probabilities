About this slice

The numbers check that the pipeline works end to end; they are not findings. Only two families of features are in: population (1959 and 1939 censuses, growth, neighbours) and administrative rank. Industry, military sites and transport come next.

A town counts as on the list when a complex or sub-complex of the SAC 1956 study (Part I, transcribed in full) lies within 10 km of it, or matches its name a little further off. Predictions are out of fold: each town was scored by a model that never saw it.

A fifth of the towns form a sealed test set, scored once at the end of the project; they carry no prediction here. Twelve towns have no label because their entries may be on a page missing from the scanned study (Astrakhan, Ashgabat, Arzamas among them).

Sources: SAC study SM 129-56 (National Security Archive, EBB 538); pop-stat.mashke.org and Demoscope (censuses); GeoNames (coordinates); Natural Earth (outlines).
